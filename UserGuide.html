CryptoScanner 6.1.0 — Nobitex / IRT

This release is a Nobitex spot scanner and paper-first trading assistant for the IRT (Rial-compatible) market.

Risk notice: no trading system guarantees profit. Profitability must be measured from paper results after fees, spread, and slippage. Never treat a signal count as proof of an edge.

1. Install

py -m venv .venv
.venv\Scripts\activate
pip install -r requirements.txt
py main.py

Before a live experiment, run the read-only Nobitex preflight:

python tools\nobitex_preflight.py

2. Credentials and IRT

Create a Nobitex API key with READ and TRADE permissions. Do not enable withdrawals. Use the encrypted local credential store or the supported environment/configuration flow; never hardcode keys.

Nobitex IRT order values remain in Rial-compatible IRT units. Toman conversion is display-only. Do not divide an order amount or price by ten.

3. The real-movement entry rule

Strict raw-scan mode does not use RSI, MACD, EMA, Bollinger Bands, Fibonacci, AI, machine learning, or price forecasting. It uses the prices observed by this scanner and a simple average of previous scans.

  1. The scanner records one valid price per symbol on every completed cycle.
  2. It waits until the configured lookback is available (6 scans by default).
  3. The current move must be strictly greater than the threshold (3% by default).
  4. The required positive streak must be present (3 consecutive positive scans by default).
  5. Recent raw prices must show higher highs and higher lows.
  6. The current price must be above the mean of previous scans, and that mean must be rising.

Negative, flat, weak, below-threshold, below-average, invalid, or unconfirmed symbols are rejected. A move exactly equal to the threshold is rejected.

Spread, quote-volume, visible depth, whitelist/blacklist, cooldown, and account-risk checks can reject a candidate for execution safety; they cannot create a trend entry.

Canonical names are threshold_percent, min_consecutive_positive_scans, trend_lookback_scans, stop_loss_percent, trailing_stop_percent, risk_per_trade_percent, cooldown_minutes, and scan_interval_seconds. The GUI accepts comma-separated symbols. History is persisted in data/scan_history.json.

5. Exits and position protection

  • Stop loss: closes a long when the observed price reaches the configured stop.
  • Trend break: closes when the shared raw assessment sees a broken lower structure or price below the previous-scan mean.
  • Trailing stop: optional; follows a new observed high after activation and never weakens below entry.
  • Risk limits: maximum positions, per-position and total exposure, minimum order value, cooldown, drawdown halt, and the existing emergency-halt path remain active.

6. Paper first

Recommended sequence: Paper → measure after costs → review → Live only with explicit activation.
  1. Leave Execution mode = Paper.
  2. Let the scanner accumulate the six-scan history and run a meaningful sample.
  3. Review net P&L, fees, spread assumptions, win rate, expectancy, maximum drawdown, and exit reasons.
  4. Do not tune from a single trade or a short sample.
  5. Only after review, switch to Live Nobitex in Bot Settings.
  6. Press Start on the Real tab. The connected account alone never arms a Nobitex BUY.
  7. Use a small allocation and verify the first fill and protective stop.

Paper and live entries are mutually exclusive. Pausing new live entries still leaves existing live positions eligible for their protective exits.

7. Measuring results

A paper report should include the test dates, scan count, candidates by rejection reason, completed trades, net P&L after fees/spread, win rate, average win/loss, expectancy, maximum drawdown, exposure, and counts of stop-loss, trend-break, and trailing exits. The application can be safe and disciplined while still being unprofitable; report the observed result honestly.

8. Troubleshooting

No entries: this is expected until enough history exists or when a symbol fails threshold, positive streak, structure, mean, whitelist, blacklist, liquidity, or risk checks.

History reset: verify the configured scan_history_file and do not delete data/scan_history.json between paper runs unless a fresh experiment is intended.

Paper mode rejects a live order: switch to Live and press Start deliberately. This guard is intentional.

Authentication or balance error: verify the key permissions, PC clock, Nobitex market, and account balance. A failed balance read is never treated as zero.
